About

Why BraidSkills Ghana?

Young people in Berekum told us that high training prices and transport costs keep them at home. We built this programme to remove those barriers.

Affordable

One fixed fee of GH₵500, so you know the cost before you join.

Close to home Updated

Training in Berekum, with a low-cost hostel planned for learners who live far away.

Built for earning

You learn braiding, pricing, customer care and how to find your first customers.

FAQs

Questions people ask

How much does it cost?

The training fee is GH₵500. The hostel, if you need it, has a separate small fee that will be confirmed.

How long is the training? Updated

3 months, 3 days a week. We will confirm the days and times before the first class.

Do I need to know braiding already?

No. Beginners are welcome. If you already braid, you can still learn pricing and how to find customers.

I live far away. Can I stay near the training? Updated

We plan a safe, low-cost hostel near the training. Learners under 18 will need a parent’s or guardian’s permission.

Is my place guaranteed if I register interest?

No. Registering interest only helps us plan. We will contact you with the next steps.
